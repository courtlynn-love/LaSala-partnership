03 — The AfroTech Experience

AfroTech

Three experiences.
One exceptional week.

During AfroTech, La Sala will host three curated experiences designed to bring together meaningful relationships, thoughtful hospitality and the energy of one of Houston's most important weeks for business, technology and culture.

Across two larger networking experiences and one intimate dinner, partners will have multiple opportunities to engage guests, host priority relationships and create a distinctive presence beyond the conference floor.

Visibility is easy.
Relevance is harder.
